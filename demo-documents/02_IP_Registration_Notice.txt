DESCRIPTION OF THE WORK
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

ProofChain is an original software system that enables users to upload
documents, anchor their cryptographic fingerprints (IPFS CIDs) on a public
blockchain, and retrieve tamper-evident proofs of authorship and timestamp.

The system is novel in the following ways:
  (a) It combines IPFS content-addressing with on-chain CID anchoring in a
      single unified pipeline, reducing user effort compared to standalone
      timestamping services.
  (b) The verification flow allows any third party to independently confirm
      a document's proof without trusting the ProofChain platform itself.
  (c) A MongoDB metadata layer separates searchable convenience data from the
      immutable on-chain proof, without compromising the integrity guarantee.
